How ₹5 / Head Pricing Works

You are only charged ₹5 per unique engaged client per calendar month. Any lead contacted via broadcast marketing, automated 24h+ follow-up, or outbound outreach is billed once per month — subsequent follow-ups or messages to the same client within that month are completely free. Billed heads remain permanently recorded even if a lead is deleted from the contact book to ensure tamper-proof ledger reconciliation.
